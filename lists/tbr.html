Written By: Zachary Kai » Published: 9 May 2026 | Updated: 9 May 2026

Every book I want to read: from long-standing wishes to 2026's catch-up list, organized by genre.

For so long I've tracked books I wish to read via Goodreads alone, despite having a perfectly suitable book blog for the last seven years. I'm remedying that here. This combines my general to-read list with 2026's catch-up project: getting around to series and authors I've adored but fallen behind on.
