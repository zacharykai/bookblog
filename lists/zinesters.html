Zinesters Directory

Written By: Zachary Kai » Published: 3 Feb 2026 | Updated: 29 Jul 2026

A directory of zine makers and their corners of the internet.

A curated directory of zine makers and their corners of the internet. Celebrating the folks keeping the spirit of zines alive.

Makers

Listed alphabetically by name.

  • Dub: a satirical take on My Little Pony exploring class disparity.
  • G.B.: instructionals on digital minimalism and geocaching.
  • Juhis: exploring board and card games and creativity.
  • Maerk: musings on older Japanese arcade games and the requiste tech.
  • Manatee: essays on everything from national parks to the web revival.
  • Mewizard: hand-drawn, collaged, and typeset perzines.
  • Notion: informationals on all things theatre.
  • Pesto Jaguar: weird, scifi-adjacent. Blends abstract imagery, comics, and text.

Submission Guidelines

To be included in the Zine Makers Directory:

  • You must make zines (print, digital, or both!)
  • You should have a personal site or online space to link to
  • That site or space should be somewhere folks can find your work or learn about you

Note: You don't need to be a prolific maker or have a huge archive. One zine is plenty.
